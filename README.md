# Bari Atelier

A luxury single-page storefront for **Bari Atelier**, an artificial jewellery boutique.

## About

Bari Atelier is built around elegant, accessible jewellery with a focus on mindful and ethical business values.

Our business is founded on:

- Debt-free business practices
- Interest-free financing
- Transparent and responsible growth
- Thoughtful jewellery selections
- A refined boutique shopping experience

## Website

The website is a mobile-first luxury storefront designed to work beautifully on phones, tablets and desktops.

### Features

- Luxury boutique-style hero section
- Emerald green, gold, baby pink and white colour palette
- Premium typography
- Animated jewellery presentation
- 6-product jewellery collection
- Product cards with pricing
- Add to Bag functionality
- Sliding shopping bag/cart
- Quantity controls
- Cart total calculation
- Responsive mobile navigation
- Checkout via WhatsApp
- Contact information through email
- Smooth scrolling and subtle animations
